about me

I’m Wasif Ahmed, a senior at LUMS majoring in electrical engineering with a minor in computer science. My research focuses on large language model alignment, reasoning, and mechanistic interpretability. I study how models represent and use information during reasoning, and whether intervening on those representations can make their behavior more reliable. I’m also exploring efficient inference, particularly speculative decoding, quantization, and compression.

Outside of research, I enjoy jogging and walking, conversations over tea, and occasional video gaming.

## RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Jan. 2026–present

Research Assistant · CITY at LUMS

For my senior thesis under Dr. Muhammad Tahir, I’m exploring the representation geometry of large language models. I compare model activations across layers and stages of reasoning to see whether particular directions or subspaces distinguish correct from incorrect reasoning traces. I’m also exploring whether similar patterns emerge in safe and unsafe responses. The goal is to test whether intervening on those representations can change the behavior they appear to track.
